# Telling Stories with Data Portfolio

Welcome to my portfolio for Telling Stories with Data at Carnegie Mellon University. This site will document my work, assignments, and final project throughout the course.

# About Me

Hi! My name is Dongdong Pan, and I am a graduate student in the Master of Information Systems Management (MISM) program at Carnegie Mellon University. My academic background is in computer science, and I am interested in using technology and data to understand and solve real-world problems.

I am particularly interested in how data can be communicated clearly to different audiences. Through this portfolio, I will document my work and progress throughout the course.

# What I Hope to Learn

In this course, I hope to learn how to turn data into clear and meaningful visual stories. I want to improve my ability to choose appropriate visualizations, communicate insights effectively, and design charts that are both informative and easy to understand.

After graduation, I hope to apply these skills in my future work, especially when communicating technical or data-driven information to people with different backgrounds.
